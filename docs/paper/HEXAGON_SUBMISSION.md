```text
Let a compact torus act on a closed manifold $X$ with isolated fixed points. The face poset $S(X)$ of the action is covered by the lattices of flats of the linear matroids of tangent weights at the fixed points. We study how much of the cohomology of $X$ these combinatorial data see.

Local equivariant cohomology. The group $H^*_T(X, X\setminus pt)$, embedded in $H^*_T(pt)$ by restriction, is the principal ideal generated by the product of the tangent weights. It determines the weights up to scalars, with multiplicities, and hence the local lattice $S(X)_{\geq pt}$.

Local matroids do not suffice. Two connected regular semisimple Hessenberg varieties in $\mathrm{Fl}(\mathbb{C}^5)$, with torus actions of complexity one, have isomorphic weight matroids at all fixed points, and hence the same Chow rings, Orlik–Solomon algebras and Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials there, but different Betti numbers.

Type A. The face poset determines the Betti numbers of every regular semisimple Hessenberg variety of type A, full or partial, and of the isospectral manifolds of Ayzenberg and Buchstaber. The reason is rigidity: the cluster-permutohedron $\mathrm{Cl}_\Gamma$ determines the graph $\Gamma$, by an elementary argument that recovers the vertices of $\Gamma$ from stars of edges.

Simply-laced types. The same holds for regular semisimple Hessenberg varieties of types A, D and E: the face poset determines the Weyl group together with the lines spanned by the roots of the Hessenberg ideal, up to isometry.

A formula. The Poincaré polynomial is a sum over the face poset, $P_X(q)=\sum_{\sigma\in S(X)}(q-1)^{\mathrm{rk}\,\sigma}e_\sigma(q)$, in which $e_\sigma$ is a sum of Tutte polynomials of the matroids of weights that are negative on a generic circle. For toric manifolds this is the $h$-vector formula.

An appendix collects the background on matroids, equivariant cohomology, GKM theory, root systems and Hessenberg varieties.
```
